## 2026-Specific Trends & Considerations

1. **Digital invitations** — eco-friendly, faster delivery, trackable RSVPs
2. **Wedding website QR codes** — print on save-the-dates
3. **Venmo/Zelle registry** — cash funds increasingly popular
4. **Sustainability** — rented/used decor, local flowers, farm-to-table
5. **Hybrid live-stream** — for guests who can't attend in person
6. **Audio guest book** — recording messages is trending
7. **AI-assisted planning** — ChatGPT for vendor outreach, timeline building
